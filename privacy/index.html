Chrome Web Store Limited Use: Synapse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. 3 How screenshots are handled When you activate Synapse on a webpage, the extension captures a screenshot of the currently visible area. This screenshot is: • Captured locally in your browser using Chrome's standard tab capture API • Transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our Vercel-hosted backend server • Passed immediately to our AI provider (Groq) for analysis • Discarded after the response is returned — it is never written to a database, file system, or log Screenshots are never stored. They exist only in memory during the brief processing window. No image of your screen is retained by Synapse or its AI providers beyond the duration of a single request. Our server does not retain request bodies. Groq's data handling is governed by their own privacy policy, which states that API inputs are not used to train models. 4 AI processing & third-party services Synapse uses the following third-party services to deliver its functionality: SERVICE PURPOSE DATA SENT Groq (primary) AI inference — answering questions using Llama 4 Screenshot images and/or selected text, subject context. If we change or add AI sub-processors, we will update this policy accordingly. Current sub-processors are listed above. 5 Payments Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Synapse never sees, handles, or stores your credit card information. When you click "Upgrade," you are redirected to a Stripe-hosted checkout page. All payment data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We receive confirmation of subscription status from Stripe (active, cancelled, trial) and store only that status — not any payment method details. 6 Data storage & retention Your email address and subscription status are retained in our Supabase database for as long as your account is active. Session answer history exists only in your browser's memory for the duration of a single browsing session — it is never sent to our servers and is cleared when you close the tab. If you cancel your subscription and request account deletion, we will remove your email and all associated records from our database within 30 days. 7 Your rights (GDPR & CCPA) You have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your account and all associated data, and withdraw consent at any time by uninstalling the extension. GDPR (EU users): If you are located in the European Union, you additionally have the right to data portability (receiving your data in a machine-readable format) and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully. CCPA (California users): If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale (we do not sell personal data).
